About YESTICH

We run a focused, customer-first online store for practical everyday products.

YESTICH LLC was formed in the State of Wyoming to sell consumer goods online. We look for products that make daily routines easier, describe them honestly, price them fairly and stand behind every order. We operate fully online and serve customers across the United States.

  • Useful products

    We only list products that solve a real everyday need at home or on the go.

  • Fair, clear prices

    The price customers see is the price they pay. No hidden fees at checkout.

  • Real support

    Every question is answered by email or phone within one business day.

What we do

We manage every part of the customer experience, from choosing products to after-sale support.

Product selection

We research products, compare options and choose items that are practical, well made and good value for money.

Online store

We run our own online store, where customers browse products, place orders and pay through a secure checkout.

Customer care

We handle order questions, delivery tracking, returns and refunds, so every customer has one place to go for help.

Product categories

General consumer goods for the home and everyday life, chosen for usefulness and value.

  • Home and living

    Storage, lighting and small décor for everyday rooms.

  • Kitchen and dining

    Tools, gadgets and accessories for cooking at home.

  • Tech accessories

    Phone stands, chargers, cables, cases and desk gear.

  • Fitness and outdoor

    Simple gear for workouts, travel and time outside.

  • Pet supplies

    Toys, bowls and grooming tools for cats and dogs.

  • Gifts

    Useful presents for birthdays, holidays and housewarmings.

We sell general consumer goods only. We don't sell age-restricted or regulated products such as tobacco, alcohol, weapons, supplements or medication.

How an order works

Every order follows the same four steps, and customers are kept updated by email at each one.

  1. 1

    Order

    The customer chooses a product and pays through a secure checkout.

  2. 2

    Confirmation

    An order confirmation is sent by email right away.

  3. 3

    Shipping

    The order is shipped and a tracking number is emailed to the customer.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    The package arrives at the customer's door. We follow up on any issue.

Our commitments

The standards we hold ourselves to with every customer and every order.

  • Accurate product descriptions and photos
  • Clear prices with no hidden fees
  • Secure checkout through established payment processors
  • Every order shipped with tracking
  • A clear, published return and refund policy
  • Replies to every message within one business day
  • Customer data handled securely and never sold
  • Following U.S. consumer protection and sales tax rules
